About me

My work

I'm a data scientist living in Boulder, CO. I currently lead the Threat Research team at Enzoic. Professionally, my interests are in data-driven applied science and engineering across many disciplines, including healthcare innovation, biology and cybersecurity, and generally any application that promotes equity and sustainability. I particularly enjoy working on interdisciplinary and translational machine learning and automation projects. I've consulted and published in various areas including fungal genome sequencing, plant pathogen analysis pipelines, and medical device development, and co-written grants funded by DARPA for over $1.2 million.

Music

I play a variety of Celtic folk instruments, mostly different forms of guitars and banjos. I play in several local groups; some of our music can be found on Bandcamp and YouTube, and I often play on Wednesday nights at the local Irish session at License No.1 in downtown Boulder. I also love synthesizers, electronic music production, and messing around with audio engineering.

Academics

I graduated from the University of Vermont with a B.A. in Ancient Greek, concentrating in Philosophy and Historiography. After working in various emergency medical services jobs for a while, I earned a post-bac in Computer Science from Tufts University, and expect to graduate with my M.S. in Data Science from the University of Colorado Boulder in 2027.

Writing

I occaisionally write non-technical essays about our interactions and relationships with technology on Medium.

Recreation

I'm out on my bike as much as possible, usually on gravel or trails, or hiking and backpacking; the photo on the home page is from a backpacking trip in the Sierras, near Bishop CA.
